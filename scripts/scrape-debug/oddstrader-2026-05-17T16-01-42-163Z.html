Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some frequently asked questions that may provide guidance for you:

Is it worth it to take a non-QB to win NFL MVP?

In the modern NFL, it’s become highly unlikely for a non-quarterback to win NFL MVP, so don’t even waste your time targeting a wide receiver or running back to take home the award. Even if there are longshot odds, it’s better to go with a quarterback instead.

Does playoff performance matter for NFL awards?

Playoff performance does not matter for NFL awards. These are based entirely on the regular season.

When is the best time to bet on NFL awards?

Think about these classic principles when betting on NFL awards: “Buy Low, Sell High.” You don’t want to be wagering on a quarterback to win NFL MVP after they just had a massive game and led their team to victory. Instead, you want to target them following a poor performance because you’ll get a better price that way.
